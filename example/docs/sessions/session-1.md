# Leaving Qarth

The caravan left Qarth before the bells. Mira had spent the night bargaining
with the salt-wardens, and came back with twelve casks of water and a debt.

---

On the second day a mule wandered off the track. Old Tobin swore it had
followed a light in the dunes; nobody else had seen one.
